Career history

  • 2024 – Present
    NHMRC EL1 Investigator — Frazer Institute, The University of Queensland

    Pipelines for spatial molecular datasets across technologies (GNT2034141); predictors of immunotherapy response in non-small cell lung cancer (MRF2031100); computational methods for high-plex, sub-cellular resolution spatial multiomics.

  • 2023 – 2024
    Senior Research Officer — Computational Systems Oncology Group, SAiGENCI, The University of Adelaide

    Spatial analysis pipelines; spatial biomarkers in lung and head & neck cancers (MRF2031100); genomic predictors of survivorship in paediatric brain cancers (GEL-E, ALCCRF).

  • 2020 – 2023
    Research Officer — Bioinformatics Division, WEHI

    Spatial and single-cell transcriptomics of drug-induced phenotypic plasticity in cancer; data analysis for the CRC for Cancer Therapeutics–Pfizer collaboration; regulatory mechanisms via data integration; multi-omics of drug response across cell lines and patients.

  • 2016 – 2020
    PhD Candidate — School of Mathematics and Statistics, The University of Melbourne

    Cancer subtype-specific gene regulatory networks; methods to quantify molecular phenotypes across cancer types; analysis of large transcriptomic datasets; development of several R packages. Supervised by Prof. Edmund Crampin and A/Prof. Melissa J. Davis.

  • 2018 – 2022
    Teaching roles — The University of Melbourne

    Workshop coordinator for BINF90002 (Elements of Bioinformatics, 2019–2022); tutor for COMP90016 (Computational Genomics, 2019) and COMP90014 (Algorithms for Functional Genomics, 2018).
